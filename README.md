# CEO RUN

Pick your CEO.

- **Jensen:** dodge the Chinese models (and the export bans). Keep the market cap up.
- **Sam:** take their RAM. Watch out for lawsuits, iris-scanning orbs and the board.

**Play it in your browser:** https://adityabatra072.github.io/ceo-run/

Built with Claude Fable 5.2. The original prompt is in [PROMPT.txt](PROMPT.txt).

- **Jump:** Space / W / ↑ / click / tap (again in the air to double jump)
- **Slide:** S / ↓ / swipe down
- **Menu:** Esc · **Sound:** M

3 lives. Best scores are saved in your browser.

Satire, obviously.
